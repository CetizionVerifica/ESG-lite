# 05 · UX: screens, flows and copy rules

Binding for every FE module. The mockups are at https://claude.ai/artifact/PUcstBvdfcWGriFXFxkte9:
- **Rows A–C:** the three designs.
- **Row E:** the editor (source in `canvas/project/E-Studio.dc.html`).

App chrome uses `src/ui` and app tokens. **Report content** (inside a sheet) uses only the renderer and the report's theme, never app styles.

## Principles
1. **Start finished.** A new report opens complete: every page laid out with real data and suggested text. Editing is refining, never building from blank.
2. **What you see is the PDF.** The sheet in the editor is the print renderer. Page view and PDF proof are one click away and never look different.
3. **Numbers are chips, not text.** Any KPI figure in text is a chip with its value (`48,620 tCO₂e`). Hover shows the KPI, year, sites and approval date. Chips can be deleted, never typed over.
4. **AI is a colleague who suggests.** Purple accent and a sparkle icon. Every suggestion is a card with **Accept**, **Try again** and **Discard**, and text suggestions show a diff. Accepted AI text keeps a small "AI" marker until someone edits it or ticks "Reviewed".
5. **Always know what's left.** A checks counter sits in the top bar. Clicking a check jumps to the page and element and offers a fix.
6. **Never lose work.** Autosave runs 1 s after the last change, with "Saved · just now" in the header. There is undo and redo (100 steps) and "Restore this version" from versions.

## S2 · New report wizard (`/new`), 4 steps, one screen each
1. **Year and scope:** the year (default: latest locked), sites (default all; site-scoped Managers see only theirs), frameworks (preselected from company setup).
2. **Design:** three large cards (Editorial / Night / Minimal) showing the cover and highlights with *this company's* brand and data (live thumbnails from `preview.html?mode=sheet&pageId=`), plus company templates if any. Under them: "Colours and fonts come from your brand. You can change anything later."
3. **Chapters:** toggles per chapter, pre-ticked from material topics and selected KPIs (Climate, Energy, Water, Waste, People, Health and safety, Diversity, Training, Human rights, Governance, Ethics), plus Highlights, Targets, Materiality (disabled with a reason when there are no scores), Data tables, GRI / BRSR / ESRS index, Methodology.
4. **Your words:**
   - leadership message: write, paste, or "Let AI draft it" (it stays unconfirmed until ticked);
   - about the company: prefilled from the profile;
   - cover photo: upload or "Generate";
   - "Write chapter text with AI" (default on).

   Then **Build report**, showing a progress list ("Laying out 42 pages… Writing climate chapter…"), then open the editor.

**Canvas interactions (RS-6):**
- Click to select. Shift-click or a marquee for multi-select. Double-click a text element to edit text (RS-7). Esc goes up one level.
- Drag to move, handles to resize (Shift keeps the ratio), a rotate handle (Shift snaps to 15°).
- Arrow keys nudge 0.5 mm; Shift+arrow nudges 5 mm.
- **Snapping:** to the page centre, the safe margin, other elements' edges and centres, and equal spacing. Guides are pink lines; Alt disables snapping.
- **Shortcuts:**
  - ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z undo and redo; ⌘C / ⌘V / ⌘D copy, paste, duplicate; ⌘G / ⇧⌘G group and ungroup; ⌘L lock;
  - Delete; ] and [ bring forward and send back;
  - ⌘+ / ⌘− / ⌘0 zoom;
  - ⌘/ shows the shortcuts.
- **Context menu:** the same actions, plus "Ask AI about this", "Copy style / Paste style", and "Replace image".
- **Locked elements** show a lock badge on hover. Template-locked ones say "Locked by the template".
- **Safe margin** is a dashed blue outline, shown while dragging or when toggled in the view menu.

**Inspector by type:**
- **Text:** style (Title / Heading / Body / Caption / Big number / Quote…), alignment, colour (brand swatches first), "Insert KPI value" (or type `{{`), AI rewrite menu.
- **KPI tile:** KPI picker (search by name and code, grouped by topic, framework badges), year, show change, show trend, variant.
- **Chart:** KPIs, years, sites, chart kind (only kinds valid for the data), grey prior years, values on bars, target line, title and caption (AI: "Suggest a title").
- **Table:** data binding or static rows, style, total row.
- **Image:** replace, fit, crop, alt text (required; AI "Write alt text"), corner radius.
- **Shape and icon:** fill, stroke, radius, opacity.
- **All:** X, Y, W, H (mm), rotation, lock, "Linked to data" badge with the approval date and evidence count.
- **Page (nothing selected):** name, background colour or image, layout it came from (with "Reset to layout"), bookmark title, "Suggest layouts (AI)".

**Flow sections (RS-7):**
- They appear as a stack of auto-generated pages with a purple "Flow" badge.
- Clicking opens their inspector instead of free placement: options (columns, KPIs, years, grouping), narrative text editor for `narrative`, and "Starts on a right-hand page".

## S4 · AI suggestions (RS-8)
- **Where:** "Ask AI" in the insert bar, the inspector's AI menu, and right-click → "Ask AI about this".
- **Suggestion card** (anchored next to the element or in the inspector):
  - the title of the action;
  - the proposal: a diff for text, a ghost preview on the sheet for elements, 2–3 thumbnails for layouts;
  - "Based on: Scope 1 FY26 · Scope 2 FY26 …" (sources);
  - the buttons **Accept**, **Try again**, **Discard**.
- **Report-wide AI** (honesty, jargon, missing disclosures) opens a findings list in the right panel. Each finding has "Go to page" and, where possible, "Suggest a fix".
- **While working:** a skeleton shimmer on the target and "Writing…". On failure: "The AI is unavailable. Your report is unchanged. [Try again]".
- **Budget:** a small meter in the AI menu ("$1.20 of $5 today"). Over budget: "AI suggestions are paused for today for this report."

## S5 · Checks and final (RS-9)
- **Checks panel:** grouped by **Must fix** (errors) / **Review** (warnings) / **For info**. Each row has the message, page and element, **Go to**, and a fix button when one exists ("Replace with linked value", "Shrink text to fit", "Move inside margin", "Open missing values").
- **Inline markers:**
  - a red wavy underline under mismatched numbers;
  - a red corner on overflowing text with a "+3 lines" tag;
  - an amber dot on AI-unreviewed text.
- **Generate final PDF** opens a confirm dialog listing the warnings with checkboxes ("I've reviewed these"), the version number and an optional note. Disabled with a reason while errors remain.
- **Progress:** a toast "Building your PDF… about a minute", then the notification "Final v3 is ready" with a Download link.

## S6 · Versions and review (`/versions`, RS-5 and RS-10)
- **Versions list:** v3 Final (download, SHA, data date, by and when), v2 Review, v1 Snapshot (open read-only, "Restore as draft").
- **Comments:** pins on elements in the editor and a thread panel. Resolving a comment hides its pin.
- **Review flow:** **Send for review** sets the status to In review. Managers see "Approve" or "Request changes"; Approve enables Generate final.
- **Data drift banner:** "4 values changed since you edited. Numbers updated automatically; 2 text passages may need a look. [Review changes]". This opens before/after cards per element.

## Copy rules
- Say "report", "page", "chapter", "design", "layout". Never "doc", "block", "binding" or "artboard".
- Name things by their purpose ("Highlights", "Climate"), not by code. Framework codes appear only as badges and on index pages.
- Write numbers in the company's format (Indian grouping when chosen) with units, and use the "▲ / ▼" convention coloured by whether the change is good (KPI `DIRECTION_GOOD`).
- Never blame the user. "This number doesn't match your approved data" beats "Invalid value".
- AI copy is plain: "Suggested by AI" and "Based on". Never "magic".
