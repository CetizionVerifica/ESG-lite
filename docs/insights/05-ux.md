# 05 · UX: the Insights page

Binding for FE lanes. Mockups of every chart: https://claude.ai/artifact/ErBXCeyw35VBWqj28Ho68h (example data). Build on the redesign system: `src/ui`, tokens, four app themes, light and dark.

## 1. Job to be done

"In thirty seconds, tell me the story of our year: are we on track, why did it move, how ready is our ESG data, and what should I do next."

Overview answers "what needs me today" (operations). Insights answers "how are we doing and why" (story). Each panel links to the working page behind it.

## 2. Principles

1. **Summary first, story second, detail on click.** Hero strip → four story panels → cards → tabs below the fold.
2. **Every number is a door.** Hero cells, bars, cells, bubbles and links open the filtered page or list that produced them (`links` in each response).
3. **Colour is never alone.** Pillars carry their letter badge (`PillarBadge`), status carries a word and an icon, change carries an arrow and the word "up"/"down" plus good/bad from `direction_good`.
4. **One axis, thin marks, a legend at 2+ series, a tooltip, a table view** (dataviz rules already used by `/kpi/esg`).
5. **Calm motion.** One orchestrated entrance per panel. Morph only between linked views. Nothing moves under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
6. **Honest states.** Each panel has its own loading skeleton, empty state (what data it needs, where to add it), error with Retry, and `kpi_off` (hidden, not empty).

┌ HeroStrip (one panel, 4 cells) ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
┌ 8/12 ─────────────────────────────────────┐ ┌ 4/12 ────────────────────────┐
│ Story 1 · Decoupling (B)                   │ │ Story 2 · ESG ring (A)       │
└────────────────────────────────────────────┘ └──────────────────────────────┘
┌ 6/12 ──────────────────────────────────────┐ ┌ 6/12 ─────────────────────────┐
│ Story 3 · Pathway (C) ⇄ Bridge (D) toggle  │ │ Story 4 · Target wall (H)     │

- **1024:** story panels stack 2-up at 6/6; hero strip 2×2.
- **768:** everything one column; ring and wall keep 320 px height.
- **390:** one column; hero cells stack; tabs become a select; the Sankey switches to its table view by default with a "Show diagram" button.
- Without KPI tracking: hero cell 3 becomes "Renewable share", Story 2 becomes "By scope and site" (treemap of sites → scopes, from the summary sources), the wall shows SBTi only, the Materiality tab is hidden, and the flow tab drops the disclosure column.

## 4. Panels

**Hero strip (IN-1).** Uses `KpiStrip`. Numbers count up from 0 over 600 ms on first load only. Net cell: value, change chip, pathway pill (On track / At risk / Off track / No target). Readiness cell: three small arcs (E S G) with letters. Needs-you cell: a count; click opens a menu of the attention items with counts, each a link.

**A · ESG ring (IN-2).** ECharts sunburst. Inner ring pillars (pillar colour, white label with letter), outer ring topics sized by KPI count, opacity 0.25 + 0.75 × coverage. Centre shows overall %. A small database icon on topics with `auto_share > 0` and a tooltip line "4 of 8 filled from your data". Click a pillar → zooms (sunburst `rootToNode`) and sets the page `pillar` filter; click centre → back. Table view: pillar, topic, KPIs, approved, coverage, from data.

**B · Decoupling (IN-2).** Three lines indexed to base = 100: output (brand), emissions (pillar E colour, light area), intensity (muted, dashed). End labels show the change ("+42%", "−19%"). Events from IN-4 appear as `markArea`/`markLine` with their label. Above the chart one sentence from the `decoupling` rule when it fires. `combined_output` → note under the chart.

**C · Pathway (IN-4).** Stacked scope bars (S1/S2/S3 tokens), dashed pathway line (ink), lever line (renewable share, accent) on a right axis 0–100 %. Gap band shaded where actual > pathway. Milestone pin at the target year. Toggle "Pathway | What moved" in the panel header switches to D with a morph (`universalTransition`, bars keep identity by year).

**D · Bridge (IN-5).** Waterfall: from-total (brand), increases (status bad), decreases (status good), to-total (brand). Labels "+5,900" / "−6,200" above bars. Driver labels in plain words: "More output", "Better process efficiency" (S1 intensity), "Less electricity per tonne", "Cleaner electricity", "Supply chain (Scope 3)", "Other". "How this is calculated" link opens a drawer with the formula and each driver's inputs.

**H · Target wall (IN-2).** Bullet bars: track (soft), progress bar (status colour), expected tick (ink). Row label: pillar letter + name; right label "58% · expected 70%". Sorted worst first. Status word in the tooltip and in the table view. Row click → `/targets` (SBTi) or the KPI target drawer (`/kpi/targets?kpi=`).

**Insight cards (IN-3).** Up to 6. Each: severity icon + word chip, one sentence, a "Show" link that scrolls to and pulses the related panel (`panel`), and "Open" for `link`. AI-reworded cards carry `AiLabel` and a "Show original" toggle.

**E · Data flow (IN-6).** ECharts Sankey, 4 columns. Left links in tCO₂e use gradient colour; right links (counts) are muted grey with style by fit (solid exact, dashed partial, dotted derived) and a legend explaining "Count of disclosures each KPI feeds". Hover a node → adjacency focus. Click a site/scope link → emissions list filtered; click a disclosure → `/kpi/reports/:framework` at that row.

**F · Materiality (IN-7).** Scatter: x impact, y financial (1–10 per scores, axis 0–10), bubble size by weight, colour by status plus the status word in the label ("Climate · at risk"). "Act here" shaded quadrant top-right (≥ 7, ≥ 7). Without scores: a list of material topics with status chips and "Add scores (Admin)" link to KPI setup.

**G · Completeness (IN-4).** Heatmap sites × months, heat ramp tokens, percentage printed in each cell (`--t-on-heat-n`). Future months are blank, not 0. Click a cell → a drawer listing missing emission entries and KPI values for that site and month with links.

**Stripes (IN-8).** One `<div>` per month coloured on the heat ramp by value between min and max; no numbers; aria-label summarises ("Monthly emissions from Jan 2021 to Sep 2026, falling 24%"). Hover shows month and value.

## 5. Interactions

- Filters live in the URL; every panel refetches on change; panels keep the previous chart visible (dimmed) while loading the next.
- Pillar filter from the ring, from the header chips, or from a wall row's letter — all set the same `pillar`.
- `?panel=bridge` scrolls to and highlights that panel (used by cards and links).
- Keyboard: Tab through panels; Enter on a panel header opens its table view; in present mode ←/→ move, Esc exits.

## 6. Motion (`src/lib/insights/motion.ts`)

- `entrance`: series draw in over 700 ms, staggered 80 ms per series, `cubicOut`. Panels fade from opacity 0.6 (never 0) to 1.
- `countUp`: hero numbers, 600 ms, first load only.
- `morph`: ECharts `universalTransition` between Pathway ⇄ Bridge, Ring ⇄ topic bars (table view toggle), 500 ms.
- `pulse`: a 2 s outline pulse on a panel targeted by `?panel=`.
- All return no-op options when `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

## 7. Copy rules

- Plain words; no disclosure codes as titles (codes only in tooltips via `FrameworkBadges`).
- Units always shown: "tCO₂e", "% of the way", "per tonne".
- Changes: "down 19% on 2025", never "−19%" alone in prose.
- Empty states say what's needed: "Add production data for 2024 and 2025 to see decoupling. Add production →".
- `kpi_off` panels are hidden; never say "KPI tracking is off" on Insights.
- Card copy templates live in `src/lib/insights/cardCopy.ts` keyed by `RuleId` (`01-contracts` §6).

## 8. Present mode (IN-8)

Full-screen route `/insights/present`. Slides: 1 hero strip with stripes band; 2 decoupling; 3 ESG ring; 4 pathway → bridge (morph on the same slide); 5 target wall; 6 top three cards. Large type (hero 56 px), same filters, brand cover colours from the theme pack. Arrow keys, a slide counter, Esc to exit.
