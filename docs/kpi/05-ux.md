# 05 · UX: how KPI tracking looks and feels (binding for every FE module)

The test for every screen: **a site contributor who has never heard of GRI, BRSR or ESRS can finish their KPIs for the month without help.** Frameworks are the Admin's and Manager's concern. A contributor answers plain questions about their site.

## Principles

1. **Enter once, report everywhere.**
   - A value is asked for once, whatever frameworks need it.
   - Small framework badges ("GRI 303-3 · BRSR P6 · ESRS E3-4") show where it's used, with a tooltip explaining each.
2. **Plain questions, not disclosure codes.**
   - Ask "How much water did Bahrain plant take in during September?", not "303-3 Water withdrawal".
   - Every question has a "What counts?" help line, plus an `InfoTip` with examples and what to exclude.
3. **Only what's yours, only what's due.**
   - A contributor sees their assigned KPIs for their sites, grouped by **Due** / **Sent back** / **Done**.
   - Nothing else: no library, no frameworks, no other sites.
4. **One thing at a time.**
   - Entry is a short card per KPI: the question, one input with its unit, and last period's value for comparison.
   - Optional parts (breakdown, evidence, comment) open only when asked for.
5. **Never type what we already know.**
   - Auto KPIs (emissions, energy, production) appear already filled, with a "From your data" chip linking to the rows they came from.
   - The contributor doesn't enter them.
6. **Never lose work.**
   - Values save as drafts as you type (`useUnsavedGuard`, `lib/entryDrafts` pattern).
   - Leaving asks only when something wasn't saved.
7. **Show progress, always.**
   - A progress bar on My KPIs ("7 of 12 done for September").
   - A ring per framework on the dashboard ("GRI 82 %").
   - A clear "All done" state.
8. **Mistakes caught gently.**
   - Unit conversion is shown as you type ("1,200 m³ = 1.2 ML").
   - A big jump from last year asks "This is 3× last September. Is that right?" with a one-line reason box. It doesn't block.
   - A breakdown that doesn't add up says by how much.
9. **Same look as the rest of ESGLite.**
   - `src/ui` components and tokens only, the four themes.
   - Status words Draft / Pending / Approved / Rejected / Missing / Locked.
   - Works at 390 px; keyboard first; dialogs trap focus.

## Screens

### S1 · Superadmin: the switch (KPI-1)
On a client's **Overview** tab, a new panel below Smart import, in the same shape as `SmartImportToggle`:
```
KPI tracking                                                     [ Off ●○ ]
Let this client track ESG KPIs for GRI, BRSR and CSRD (ESRS). Their Admins choose
frameworks and KPIs; their users enter values beside their emission data.
```
- Turning it on shows a toast: "KPI tracking is on for Midal Cables. Their Admins will see KPI setup."
- Turning it off asks for confirmation: "Hide KPI tracking? Their values are kept and come back if you turn it on again."

### S2 · Admin: setup wizard (KPI-3), `/kpi/setup`, `Stepper` with 4 steps, resumable
1. **Frameworks**
   - Three cards: GRI, BRSR, CSRD/ESRS. Each has one line on who it's for, a "Recommended for you" hint (e.g. BRSR for Indian listed companies) and a version picker.
   - Pick the reporting year (CY/FY, prefilled).
2. **What matters to you** (only if ESRS is picked)
   - Ten topic rows, E1 Climate … G1 Business conduct, each a Yes/No toggle.
   - "No" asks for a one-line reason, since ESRS requires it.
   - Climate is preselected Yes, with a note.
3. **KPIs**
   - A list grouped by topic, **pre-ticked** with what the chosen frameworks require.
   - Each row shows: the plain name; the badges; "Auto" or "Calculated" chips; and the level (site or company) with the frequency.
   - Unticking a required KPI asks for a reason. "Add your own KPI" opens a drawer: name, question, unit, frequency, level, optional mapping (with AI-suggested mappings, KPI-12).
   - A live count shows "34 KPIs · 12 filled automatically · 22 to enter".
4. **Who enters what**
   - A matrix of KPIs (rows) × sites (columns), defaulted to each site's existing data-entry user (from `user.site`) and the site's manager as reviewer.
   - "Apply to all sites" per row. Company-level KPIs go to the Admin by default.

Finish shows a summary card and "Invite your team to enter KPIs" (a notification to assigned owners). Every step can be revisited later from Settings → KPI setup.

- "Save and next" moves to the next due KPI.
- **Submit all** sends every completed draft for the period in one action, with a summary ("Send 4 values to Mia (Manager) for review?").
- When the company's switch is off this page isn't reachable, and My month shows no KPI card.

**Fast lane for many values:** a "Table view" toggle shows every due KPI × period in a grid, with keyboard entry (Tab / Enter) and the same checks. Plus "Download template / Upload filled template" (Excel), which reuses bulk-upload patterns.

### S4 · Manager: review (KPI-8), Approvals → **KPIs** tab
- The same layout as emission approvals: **Ready (n)** / **Needs attention (n)**, with reason chips (`KPI_CHANGE_LIMIT` "3× last year", `KPI_EVIDENCE` "No evidence").
- The record drawer shows the question, the value, the unit conversion, the breakdown, evidence, last periods (a mini chart) and history.
- Actions: **Approve all ready** (with an 8-second undo), plus Approve / Reject with reason chips one at a time.
- **Close period** (the `CloseMonthView` pattern): what's left per site, then Lock.

### S5 · Dashboard (KPI-10), `/kpi/dashboard`
- **Top row:** one ring per enabled framework, e.g. "GRI · 82 % · 6 gaps", "BRSR · 74 % · Core 9/9".
- **Below:** KPI cards by topic. Each card shows the value this year, the change on last year, a sparkline, target progress (KPI-9) and a status dot.
- **Gaps list:** "Water discharged · Matola · 3 months missing → Ask Ravi", with a link that sends a reminder or opens the value.
- **Filters:** year, site and topic (`ReportFilters`).

### S6 · Framework reports (KPI-11), Reports → Framework reports
- One tab per framework.
- The **GRI content index** table lists Disclosure / Value / Unit / Where (site or company) / Notes, then omissions with their reason.
- **BRSR** shows Principle 6 / 3 / 5 … tables in SEBI's layout, with current and previous year.
- **ESRS** shows datapoints by topic, with not-material topics listed with their reason.
- Buttons: **Download XLSX**, **Download PDF** (GRI). A banner reads "Only approved values · 3 values pending review", linked to them.

### S7 · Superadmin: KPI library (KPI-2), Setup → KPI library
- A table of KPIs with filters (framework, topic, source).
- A KPI drawer with: definition, unit, aggregation, formula (with a live check), and a mappings table (framework, disclosure, fit, display unit, note).
- A disclosures view per framework version, showing coverage ("ESRS E1: 14 of 18 datapoints mapped").

## Copy rules
- **Sentence case.** Use "tCO₂e", "m³" and "GJ" with proper symbols. Numbers use the locale format with the unit after the value.
- **Framework names:**
  - "GRI", "BRSR" (Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report), "CSRD (ESRS)" in setup.
  - "ESRS" in badges and reports.
  - Disclosure codes appear only in badges, tooltips and reports, never as the question.
- **Status words:** Draft / Pending / Approved / Rejected / Missing / Locked.
- **Errors and empty states:** say what to do next ("No KPIs are assigned to you yet. Your admin chooses them in KPI setup."). No raw codes in the UI.
