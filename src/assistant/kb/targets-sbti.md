[Targets (SBTi)](/targets) works out a science-based target for your sites and shows, year by year, whether you're on the pathway. SBTi is the Science Based Targets initiative. Only Managers can open this page, for their own sites.

**Set a target**
1. Open **Targets (SBTi)**. All your sites are included. Use the **Site** chip to pick fewer.
2. Choose the **Base year**, 2015 or later. It starts on your company's Scope 3 base year if one is set, otherwise last year.
3. Under **Target**, choose **Near-term** or **Net-zero 2050**.
4. For **Near-term**, choose a **Horizon** of 5 or 10 years and a **Pathway**: **1.5°C · 4.2%/yr** or **WB2°C · 2.5%/yr**.

The page updates as you choose.

**Reading the page**
- The top row shows **Base emissions**, **Target**, **Latest actual vs pathway**, **Annual rate** and **Years left**.
- **SBTi rules check** tests your setup and says what to change, such as adding every site to cover Scope 1+2.
- If Scope 3 is 40% or more of base-year emissions, it needs a target and is included in the pathway.
- The tabs are **Pathway table**, **Scope-wise** and **Actual vs target**. The last one marks each year **Reached**, **Not reached** or **No data**.

Every figure uses Approved entries only.

**"No data for base year"**
The chosen sites have no approved emissions for that year. Choose a year offered, such as **Use 2023**, or check [Approvals](/data/approvals) for entries still waiting.

**What this page doesn't do**
- It doesn't save a target. Your choices live only in the page's web address, so nobody else sees them unless you share the link. To keep a copy, choose **Export PDF** or **Export XLSX**.
- It sends nothing to SBTi, and a clean rules check isn't SBTi's approval.
- It isn't the same as KPI targets, which are under **KPIs** › [Targets](/kpi/targets) if KPI tracking is on.
