A site is one location of a client, such as a plant or an office. Data is always entered for a site, and each site has its own list of categories. Only a Superadmin can add, edit or delete sites. Admins see their company's sites on the [Users](/users) page and ask their Superadmin for changes.

**Add a site (Superadmin)**
1. Open **Setup** › [Sites](/setup/sites) and choose **Add site**.
2. On **Details**, enter the **Site name**, **Address** and **Contact person**, and choose the **Client** and **Country**.
3. On **Categories**, tick the categories this site reports. Search, **Select all** and **Clear all** help with long lists.
4. Save.

**Edit a site**
Select it in the list. The drawer has these tabs:
- **Details** and **Categories**, as above.
- **People**: the users and managers on the site.
- **Capture**: which of its categories have a data-entry form.
- **Lease**: lease type and floor area, used for floor-area methods in Categories 8 and 13.

The list shows each site's categories by scope, its users and managers, and **Config coverage**: how many of its categories have a form.

**After adding categories**
Contributors can enter data for a category only once it has a data-entry form. A site with no manager shows "No manager looks after this site yet".

**Delete a site**
Choose **Delete site**. The confirmation lists what goes with it, including its emission entries and their evidence, its factors and site-specific units, products and production data, uploaded bills, KPI values, period locks and site mappings. People assigned to the site keep their accounts but lose access to it; anyone left with no site needs one assigned in Users.
