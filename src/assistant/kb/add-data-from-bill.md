**Start from a bill** lets the AI read a bill (PDF or photo) and fill in the rows for you. You check every row before anything is saved.

1. In [Add data](/data/new), choose the site, category and period, then **Continue**.
2. Open the **Start from a bill** tab.
3. Drop your bills into **Bills** (PDF or photo, up to 10 MB each). You can add several.
4. Wait while each bill is read. Its rows appear next to a preview of the bill, grouped by bill.
5. Check each row. Fields the AI filled carry an **AI** mark. If the AI isn't sure about a row's category, the row says so: check it carefully.
6. Correct anything that's wrong. Choose **Add item from this bill** for a line the AI missed.
7. When a bill's rows are right, choose **Use this row** or **Use these N rows**.
8. Choose **Review**, then send as usual.

Nothing from a bill is sent until you've confirmed it. If you try to review too early, ESGLite tells you which bills aren't checked yet.

**Messages you might see**
- "Wrong period for this bill": the bill is dated outside the period you picked. Choose **Remove bill**, then add it again with its own period selected.
- "The AI flagged this bill": for example, the total doesn't match subtotal plus tax. Check every value.
- "We couldn't read this bill": type the values in on the **Type it in** tab, or try a clearer PDF or photo.

**Evidence**
The bill is attached to the saved rows as evidence automatically.

**My bills**
Choose **My bills** to see the bills uploaded for this site and category. You can **Preview** one, **Read again** to reuse it, or **Delete bill**. Deleting a bill doesn't take it off entries already saved from it.

You can mix typed rows and bill rows in the same send.
