ESGLite has two emissions reports, and both use Approved data only. Managers open them from **Reports**. Superadmins open them from **Reports** and pick the client with the client switcher. Admins don't have these reports in their menu.

**GHG report**
[GHG report](/reports/ghg) compares a period with the one before it.
1. Set the header filters: **Sites**, **Categories**, **Calendar** (**CY** or **FY**), **Frequency** (**Year**, **Quarter** or **Month**) and **Year**, plus the quarter or month.
2. The results update as you change them.
3. The summary shows total emissions, Scope 1, Scope 2, Scope 3, data coverage and the largest source.
4. The tabs are **Summary**, **By location**, **Scope 1**, **Scope 2**, **Scope 3**, **Methodology** and **Findings**.
5. Choose **Download branded PDF** for a PDF in your company's look, or **Export tables** for the tables.

If Design Studio is on for your company, **Design this report** opens it there.

**EDE report**
[EDE report](/reports/ede) is the operational view: which site emits what, month by month, with renewables produced, emissions saved and intensity. It works on calendar years, by **Year** or **Month**. Choose **Download PDF** for a PDF.

**The report is empty or lower than expected**
Reports count Approved entries only. Entries that are still Pending or were Rejected aren't included. Managers can check [Approvals](/data/approvals) for entries waiting.

**Other report pages**
- **Framework reports** (GRI and others), if KPI tracking is on.
- **Scope 3 boundary**, which shows what's in and out of Scope 3.
