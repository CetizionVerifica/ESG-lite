**Settings** holds your profile, the look of the app, your email notifications and your timezone. Open it from the avatar menu in the top bar, or go to [Settings](/settings).

**Profile**
Shows your name, last name, phone, email, role and sites as your administrator set them up. You can't change these here; ask your administrator. To change your password, choose **Email me a reset link** and follow the email (at least 8 characters).

**Appearance**
Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System** (follows your device). It's saved to your account, so it follows you to other devices. You can also switch it from the avatar menu under **Appearance**. Your organisation sets its theme (colours and logo) for everyone.

**Notifications**
Switch the emails for your role on or off:
- Users: **Approvals**, **Rejections**, **Deadline reminders**.
- Managers: **Escalations**, **Uploads to review**.
- Admins: **Uploads to review**.
- Superadmins have no email options yet.

In-app notifications are always on.

**Timezone**
Search by city or region and pick your timezone. Reminders are sent at 8:00 AM your time, and [Current Month](/my-month) changes month at your midnight.

**Scope 3** (Admins and Superadmins)
A link to the company's Scope 3 boundary.

**Connected apps** (Superadmins)
Apps such as Claude that are allowed to act for your account, with **Disconnect** to stop their access.
