Onboarding creates a client company, its company admin and a main site. Only a Superadmin can do it, and you can change any of it later.

Open **Clients** › [Onboard client](/clients/new), or choose **Onboard client** on the [Console](/console).

**The steps**
1. **Company**: only **Company name** is required. The **Address** is also used for the client's first site.
2. **Admin user**: enter the **Contact name** and **Email**. This person becomes the client's company admin and can add the rest of their team. Then either switch on **Email an invite so the admin chooses their own password** (the link works for 7 days), or set a **Password** of at least 8 characters and share it safely.
3. **Access**: switch on **This client can use ESG-Mitra** if it applies. The step also shows the **Reporting calendar**; the financial year is the same for every client today.
4. **Brand** (optional): add a **Logo**, **Logo for dark backgrounds (optional)**, **Colour guideline (optional)**, **Primary** and **Accent**. **Suggest from logo** picks colours from the logo. **Skip for now** leaves the client on the PlanetPulse theme.
5. **Start from** (optional): **Start empty**, **An existing client** or **A setup file**. The last two copy forms, units, factors, mappings and a threshold onto the new client.
6. **Review**: check each section, use **Edit** to go back, then choose **Create client**.

**After you create it**
The client gets one site named "(client name) - Main Site". The next page lists what to do next, with a link for each:
- If you started empty: **Add sites**, **Assign categories**, **Configure capture**, **Load factors**.
- If you copied a setup: **Check sites**, **Check forms**, **Check factors**, **Add people**.

Anything that failed shows under "Some things didn't go through". If the invite wasn't sent, the admin can't sign in yet: open the client's **People** tab and choose **Send invite**.

Every feature switch starts off. Turn on what this client needs on its **Overview** tab.
