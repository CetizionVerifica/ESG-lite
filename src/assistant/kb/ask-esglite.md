**Ask ESGLite** is the small bubble at the bottom right of every page. Ask how to do something and it answers from ESGLite's help articles. It can't see your company's data, and it never saves, approves or changes anything.

**Show it a file**
If your company has this turned on, you'll see a paperclip next to the question box. To attach a file, choose the paperclip, drop the file on the panel, or paste a picture.

Photos, PDFs (scanned ones too), Excel, CSV, Word and PowerPoint files can be read. With any other file it goes by the name.

Then you can ask things like:
- Where should I upload this?
- What does this say?
- Is this accurate?

**What it can't tell you**
It can't confirm that a document is true or that its figures are right. It tells you what it can see, what looks missing or unclear, and what to double-check. A person's review decides.

**Your file**
Your file is sent to an outside AI service to be read, and ESGLite doesn't save it. Attaching it here doesn't enter any data: to add it, use the steps it gives you.

If you don't see the paperclip, attaching files isn't turned on for your company. ESGLite turns it on, not your company's settings, so ask your ESGLite contact.

**Other tips**
- You can ask in your own language.
- Choose the thumbs up or down under an answer to say whether it helped.
