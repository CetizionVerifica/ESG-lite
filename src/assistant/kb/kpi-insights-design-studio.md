KPI tracking, Insights and Design Studio are extra modules. Each one shows only when a Superadmin has switched it on for your company. While it's off, its menu items are hidden and a link to it says it isn't available. To have a module turned on, ask your Superadmin.

**KPI tracking**
Tracks ESG figures beyond emissions, such as water, people or governance, against frameworks such as GRI, BRSR and ESRS.
- Users: [My KPIs](/kpi) lists the plain questions you've been given for each site and period. Your answers save as drafts as you type ("Draft saved."); send them with **Submit**. Values marked "From your data" fill in by themselves from approved data.
- Managers: the **KPIs** menu has **ESG dashboard**, **Dashboard** and **Targets**. Submitted values are reviewed in Approvals on the **KPIs** tab. **Framework reports** is under **Reports**.
- Admins: **KPI setup** chooses what your company reports and who enters it. Admins also have the dashboards, **Targets** and **Framework reports**.
- Superadmins keep the **KPI library** that every client picks from.

**Insights** (Managers, Admins, Superadmins)
[Insights](/insights) shows how you're doing and why: emissions, targets and ESG data in one story, from approved data. Managers see their own sites.

**Design Studio** (Managers, Admins, Superadmins)
[Design Studio](/studio) designs GHG and sustainability reports from your approved data; every number stays linked to it. Choose **New design** to start, and **Brand kit** for your brand's look. Sustainability designs also need KPI tracking.

Users don't see Insights or Design Studio.
