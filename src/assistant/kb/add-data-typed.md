**Add data** is where you record your numbers for one site, category and period and send them to your manager for approval. Only Users (contributors) have this page.

**Step 1 · What are you reporting?**
1. Open [Add data](/data/new).
2. Choose the **Site** (shown only if you have more than one) and the **Category**.
3. Under **Period**, choose **Monthly** and a **Month**, or **Yearly**, then **Calendar year** or **Financial year**, and a **Year**.
4. Choose **Continue**.

You only see the categories you've been given. If you see "This category isn't set up for data entry yet", ask your admin to set up its form.

**Step 2 · Enter values**
1. Stay on the **Type it in** tab.
2. Fill in the row: the **Emission category** (what the row is, for example a fuel), the amount and the **Unit**. Open **More details** for extra fields.
3. The row shows its total, "= … tCO₂e", as you type.
4. Choose **Add row** for another line (or press Enter in a field). Use **Duplicate** or **Remove** on a row as needed.
5. Optionally attach the bill under **Evidence**.
6. Choose **Review** (or press Ctrl+Enter).

Rows with a problem are marked in red. Fix them, then continue.

**Step 3 · Review & submit**
Check the rows, the total tCO₂e and the evidence, then choose **Submit for approval**. Each row's status changes to **Sent**.

If your company uses the review workflow, you see **Save as draft** and **Save and submit** instead. Drafts stay private to you until you submit them from [Current Month](/my-month).

**What happens next**
Your rows show as Pending in [My entries](/data/mine) until your manager approves them or sends them back with a reason. The success screen offers **Add another category**, **Back to Current Month** and **View in My entries**.

Below the steps, a list shows what's already entered for the same site, category and period, with **Edit** (Pending) or **Fix** (Rejected).

If you leave with rows not sent, they stay as a draft on this device; files you picked are dropped.
