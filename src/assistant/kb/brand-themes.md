A brand theme gives a client its own name, logos and colours. Only a Superadmin can change it. The client's Admins see it on their **Brand theme** page, view only.

**Open it**
- Pick the client in the client switcher, then choose **Clients** › **Brand themes**. With no client picked, this opens the Companies list.
- Or open the client in [Companies](/clients), go to its **Brand theme** tab and choose **Open brand theme** (**Set up brand theme** if it has none yet).

**Edit the theme**
1. **Display name**: shown in the top bar, on sign-in and on reports.
2. **Logo (light background)** and **Logo (dark background)**: PNG, JPG, SVG or WebP, up to 5 MB. The dark one is a white or one-colour logo.
3. **Primary** and **Accent**. **Suggest from logo** offers colours; choose **Use as primary** or **Use as accent**.
4. **Cover gradient from** and **Cover gradient to**.
5. **Scope 3 colour**, if you want a custom one.
6. **Default look**: **Classic**, **Light** or **Night**. Anyone who picks dark appearance gets Night.
7. Check the preview: **Overview**, **Sign in**, **Report cover** and **Email**.
8. Choose **Save theme**. Client users see it the next time they open ESGLite.

**Contrast report**
Below the preview, this says how many colour pairs pass AA. If a pair can't be fixed, you see **Save is blocked**: pick a different colour.

**Other actions**
- **Discard changes** drops your edits.
- **Reset to PlanetPulse** puts the colours, cover and default look back. The name and logos stay, and nothing is saved until you choose **Save theme**.
- **Branded report**: pick a **Year** and choose **Open PDF** to see the GHG report PDF as the client gets it. It uses the saved theme.
- The client's **Brand theme** tab also holds a colour guideline file: PDF, PNG, JPG or WEBP, up to 10 MB.

With Design Studio on, report fonts, palettes and brand voice are set in the [Brand kit](/studio/brand-kit).
