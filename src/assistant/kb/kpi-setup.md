[KPI setup](/kpi/setup) turns the frameworks your company reports into the KPIs people owe. Find it under **KPIs** › **KPI setup**. It needs KPI tracking switched on, which only a Superadmin can do. Admins set up their own company; a Superadmin picks the client first.

**The first time: four steps**
Each step saves when you go on, so you can stop and come back.
1. **Frameworks**: under **Which frameworks do you report?**, tick **GRI**, **BRSR** or **CSRD (ESRS)**, and the version if there's a choice. Set **Reporting year**, **First year to track** and **Values are due on day** (1 to 28, of the month after each period ends).
2. **What matters to you**: only for CSRD (ESRS). Answer every topic. A topic that doesn't matter needs a one-line reason.
3. **KPIs**: tick what you report. Leaving out a required KPI asks for a **Reason**, which is shown in your reports. **Add your own KPI** adds one for your company.
4. **Who enters what**: under **By topic**, pick one person per topic and site. They see every KPI of that topic on My KPIs, and KPIs you add later go to them too. **Same person for every site** saves time. **Exceptions** sends one KPI to someone else. Values go to the site's managers for review.

Choose **Finish setup**. Then **Invite your team to enter KPIs** tells each person what they enter. **Change setup** goes back to the steps.

**Changing it later**
Once setup is finished, the same steps show as tabs. Change one and choose **Save changes**.

**If it says "Add a site first"**
The company has no sites yet. Admins can't add sites, so ask your Superadmin. A Superadmin adds them under **Setup** › **Sites**.
