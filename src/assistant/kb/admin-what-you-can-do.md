An Admin looks after one client company. You belong to the company, not to a site, and you work across all its sites. You start on [Users](/users).

**What you can do**
- [Users](/users): invite Managers and Users, change their role and sites, send a reset link, or remove someone.
- [Bulk upload](/capture/upload): upload rows for any site of your company. They wait for a Manager's approval.
- [Scope 3 boundary](/settings/scope3): change what's in and out of Scope 3. Managers can only read it.
- [Suppliers](/suppliers): add suppliers and keep their figures.
- [Brand theme](/brand): see your theme. It's view only.
- [Overview](/overview): read your company's figures and, under **Submission status**, see who hasn't sent a month's data. It isn't in your menu, and it's read-only.

If your company has them switched on:
- [Insights](/insights).
- **KPIs**: **ESG dashboard**, **Dashboard**, **Targets** (you can set them), **Framework reports** and **KPI setup**. If you're given KPIs to enter, open [My KPIs](/kpi); it isn't in your menu.
- [Design Studio](/studio): design reports. Only a Manager can make the final or create a view link.

**What you can't do**
- Approve or reject anything. **Approvals** and the **Emissions ledger** are for Managers, who review entries and KPI values.
- Type in entries. **Add data** and **My entries** are for Users; you use Bulk upload.
- Open the **GHG report** or **EDE report**. Ask a Manager, or ask your Superadmin.
- Add, edit or delete sites. Your sites show on Users; a Superadmin changes them.
- Invite another Admin. You can invite a **Manager** or a **User (contributor)**; a Superadmin sets up Admins.
- Change your brand theme, emission factors, category mappings, data-entry forms or thresholds, or turn a module on. A Superadmin does these.
- Open **Team**, **Targets (SBTi)**, **Products (PCF)** or **Production data**. These are Manager pages.

**"You don't have access to this page"**
Your role can't open that page, so this isn't a fault on your account. Ask your Superadmin if you need something from it.
