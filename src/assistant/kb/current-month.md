[Current Month](/my-month) is your home page. It shows what you owe for the month that's due, what was sent back, and how long you have.

**Which month**
It opens on last month, because data for a month is due on the 10th of the next month. Use **Previous month** and **Next month** to move. If you work on several sites, pick one under **Site**.

**The due banner**
For example "Due in 4 days (10 Oct). 6 of 9 categories filed." After the 10th it reads "Overdue since …" and says when it goes to your manager. After the 15th it reads "Escalated to your manager".

**Sent back to you**
Entries your manager rejected are listed on top with the reason. Choose **Fix** to open the entry in Add data, correct it and send it again.

**Checklist**
Your categories, grouped by Scope 1, 2 and 3, each with its status:
- **To do**: nothing filed yet. Choose **Add** to open [Add data](/data/new) with the site, category and month filled in.
- **Pending**, **Approved** or **Rejected**: choose the arrow (›) to see the entries.

A colleague's entry for the same site and category counts too. Months covered by an approved yearly entry aren't asked for again.

**Your month at a glance**
Totals for what you've entered, what's pending and what's approved.

**If your company uses the review workflow**
- **Drafts**: rows you saved as drafts. Only you can see them. Tick them and choose **Submit N rows** to send them for approval.
- **Nothing this month**: for a monthly category with nothing to report, choose it and confirm. Your manager sees it marked. **Undo** takes it back.
- **Missing**: a category still to do after the due date.
- **Locked**: your manager has closed the month, so it can't change until it's reopened.

When every category is in, you see "All N categories are in. Nice work."
