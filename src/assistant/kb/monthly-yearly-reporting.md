Every category can be reported **monthly** (one period per month) or **yearly** (one entry for the whole year).

**Choosing yearly**
In [Add data](/data/new) step 1, or the Upload step of [Bulk upload](/capture/upload), under **Period** choose **Yearly**, then:
- **Calendar year**: January to December.
- **Financial year**: April to March.

Then pick the **Year**. A yearly entry covers every month of its year. You can only file for a month or year that has already started.

**The mode lock**
Within one year, a category at a site is filed either monthly or yearly, never both. Mixing them would count the same months twice.
- If the category already has monthly entries for that year, a yearly entry is refused: "This category is already filed monthly for …".
- If it already has yearly data, a monthly entry is refused: "This category already has yearly data for …".
- A calendar year and a financial year overlap by nine months, so one category can't use both for overlapping periods.

Add data warns you at the top of step 2 when the period is already filed the other way. In the review step those rows read "Can't be sent: this period is filed the other way". Changes to entries that are already saved can still be sent.

**How to switch**
Keep entering the category the way it's already filed, or have the existing entries deleted first. Users can't delete entries: ask your manager, who can delete them in the Emissions ledger.

**In Current Month**
Once a yearly entry is approved, [Current Month](/my-month) counts the other months of that year as covered by the yearly filing, so you aren't asked for monthly data. Yearly rows are labelled, for example, "Yearly · FY 2025-26".

**In reports**
The GHG report can show a calendar year (**CY**) or a financial year (**FY**).
