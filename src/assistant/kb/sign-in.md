You sign in to ESGLite with your company email and either your password or a one-time code sent by email.

**Sign in with a password**
1. Go to the [sign-in page](/login).
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**, then choose **Sign in**.

ESGLite staff (Superadmins) use the [staff sign-in](/superadmin/login) instead. If you use the wrong page, ESGLite tells you which one to use and signs you out.

**Sign in with a code (no password needed)**
1. On the sign-in page choose **Email me a sign-in code**.
2. Enter your email and choose **Send code**.
3. Type the 6-digit code from the email into **Sign-in code**, then choose **Sign in**.

A code expires in 10 minutes and works once. After 5 wrong tries it stops working. **Send a new code** replaces the old one, so only the newest code works. **Use a different email** starts again.

**Forgot your password**
1. On the sign-in page choose **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your email and choose **Send reset link**.
3. Open the link in the email. Type a **New password** (at least 8 characters) and type it again in **Confirm new password**.

The same panel also offers **Email me a sign-in code** if you want to get in straight away.

For privacy, ESGLite never says whether an email address has an account. If no email arrives, check your spam folder, then ask your administrator to check the address on your account.

Reset links work once and expire. If yours has expired, ask for a new one from the sign-in page.

**Change your password while signed in**
Open the avatar menu, choose **Settings**, and under **Profile** choose **Email me a reset link**.

**New account**
Your administrator either emails you a link to choose your own password (it expires in 1 hour) or gives you a temporary password.
