ESGLite tells you about approvals, rejections, deadlines and uploads, in the app and by email.

**The bell**
The bell in the top bar shows your latest unread notifications. Choose one to open it, **Mark all read** to clear them, or **See all** for the [Notifications](/notifications) page. Its tabs filter by **All**, **Unread**, **Approvals**, **Rejections**, **Reminders** and **Uploads**.

**Monthly deadline**
Data for a month is due on the 10th of the next month.
- **10th, reminder**: at 8:00 AM your time, each User who still has a category with nothing filed for last month gets one email and a "Submission Reminder" notification naming the site. A colleague's entry for the same site and category counts as filed.
- **15th, escalation**: at 8:00 AM their time, each site's Managers get one email and a "Pending Submissions" notification saying who still hasn't filed.

[Current Month](/my-month) shows the same dates in its due banner.

**Approvals and rejections**
When a manager approves or rejects your entries, you get a notification and an email. A rejection includes the reason.

**Uploads**
Managers are told when a batch of uploaded rows is waiting for their review. Admins are told when rows are uploaded for a site with no manager.

**Choose your emails**
1. Open the avatar menu and choose **Settings**.
2. Under **Notifications**, switch the emails for your role on or off:
   - Users: **Approvals**, **Rejections**, **Deadline reminders**.
   - Managers: **Escalations**, **Uploads to review**.
   - Admins: **Uploads to review**.

In-app notifications are always on.

**Set your timezone**
Reminders follow your timezone. Set it in **Settings** under **Timezone**. If none is set, UTC is used.
