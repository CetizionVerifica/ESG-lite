Admins manage their company's people in [Users](/users); the page is called **People**. Superadmins manage everyone in **Setup** › [Users](/setup/users).

**Roles**
- **User (contributor)**: enters data for their sites and categories.
- **Manager**: approves and rejects data for their sites, and chooses which categories each User can enter.
- **Admin**: manages one client's people. An Admin belongs to a client, not to sites; Superadmins set Admins up.
- **Superadmin**: ESGLite staff.

**Invite someone (Admin)**
1. Choose **Invite person**.
2. Enter the **Name** and **Email**, choose the **Role** (**Manager** or **User (contributor)**) and one or more **Sites**.
3. Set a **Temporary password** (at least 8 characters, never shown again). You can also send them a set-password link, which expires in 1 hour.
4. Save.

**Change someone**
Open the menu on their row and choose **Edit** to change their role or sites.

Changing a person's sites resets their category access to every category on the chosen sites. Their Manager can narrow it again on the **Team** page.

The same menu has **Send reset link** (emails a link to set a new password) and **Remove…**. Someone with entries or approvals on record can't be removed; nothing changes.

The top of the page shows **Sites without a manager**, so you can spot sites nobody reviews.

**Superadmin extras**
The Superadmin's drawer also has **Last name**, **Phone**, **Timezone**, **Client** and **Categories**. A client's account can only hold sites of that one client; an **Internal account** (your team's own login) can have sites of several clients. When you change sites here, categories still available stay as they are and the new sites' categories are switched on. New people get a temporary password, shown once after you add them.
