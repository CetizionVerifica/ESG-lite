The [Team](/team) page shows who reports data on your sites and which categories each person can enter.

**The list**
Each person shows their sites, how many categories they have, and **This month**: **Submitted** or **Missing** for the month that's due. Search by name, email or site, or filter by site.

**Change someone's categories**
1. Choose **Manage** on their row. "Category access — {name}" opens, with one section per site.
2. Switch each category on (**Enabled**) or off (**Revoked**). **Enable all** and **Disable all** change a whole site.
3. Choose **Save**. You'll see "Access updated for {name}".

**Things to know**
- Access is set per category, not per site. If a category is on two of the person's sites, switching it changes both.
- Keep at least one category enabled. With none, the person gets every category.
- Only categories set up for the site are listed.
- You can't add people or change their sites here; ask your company Admin. When an Admin changes someone's sites, their categories reset to every category on those sites, so check them again here.
