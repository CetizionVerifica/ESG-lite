**Close month** locks a month once all its data is in, so reports read it as final. It's a tab in [Approvals](/data/approvals) and appears only when your company uses the review workflow.

**What the tab shows**
- **Completeness per site**: for each of your sites, how many owed categories are done, the counts of Draft, Pending, Rejected and Missing, and whether the month is **Open** or Locked.
- A Scope 3 grid: each Scope 3 category's boundary status and whether data is present.
- **Left before you can lock**: every site and category still missing something, with links to sort it out.

**Lock a month**
1. Open the **Close month** tab and pick the month.
2. Work through **Left before you can lock** until it's empty. A category a contributor marked "Nothing this month" counts as done.
3. Choose **Check again** to refresh the list.
4. Choose the lock button, for example **Lock Sep 2026** or **Lock Sep 2026 for 3 sites**.

The lock button stays off until nothing is left, with the reason beside it. Once locked, the month reads "Locked. Reports read this month as final." Nobody can add, edit, approve, reject or delete data for that site and month.

**Reopen a month**
1. Choose **Reopen** on the site's row.
2. Give a reason of at least 5 characters. It's kept in the audit trail.
3. Confirm. Contributors can change and submit data again until the month is locked again.
