ESGLite records your company's activity data (fuel, electricity, travel and more), works out the emissions in tCO₂e and gets them approved for reporting.

**How data flows**
1. A User enters data and sends it for approval. It shows as Pending.
2. A Manager approves it, or rejects it with a reason. A Rejected entry goes back to the User to fix.
3. Reports read Approved data only.

**The four roles**
- **User** (contributor): enters data for their sites and categories. Starts on [Current Month](/my-month). Menu: **Current Month**, **Add data**, **My entries**, **Production**.
- **Manager**: reviews data for their sites. Starts on [Overview](/overview), with totals and a "Needs your attention" list. Menu: **Overview**, **Data** (Approvals, Emissions ledger, Production data, Suppliers, Bulk upload), **Reports**, **Targets (SBTi)**, **Products (PCF)**, **Team**.
- **Admin**: manages one client company's people. Starts on [Users](/users). The menu also has **Bulk upload**, **Brand theme**, **Scope 3 boundary** and **Suppliers**.
- **Superadmin**: ESGLite staff who run the app for every client. Starts on [Console](/console). Menus: **Clients**, **Setup**, **Factors**, **Capture**, **Reports**. The client switcher in the page header picks which client a page shows.

Some items appear only when your company has them switched on: KPI tracking, Insights and Design Studio.

**Getting around**
- Press Ctrl K (⌘K on a Mac), or use the search box in the top bar, to jump to any page.
- The bell shows your latest notifications.
- The avatar menu has **Appearance**, **Settings** and **Sign out**.
- On a small screen, open the menu with the menu button at the top left.

**"You don't have access to this page"**
Your role can't open that page. If you need it, ask your company admin to change your access.
