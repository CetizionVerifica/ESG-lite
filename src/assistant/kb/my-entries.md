[My entries](/data/mine) lists everything submitted for your sites, with its status, so you can follow progress and fix what was sent back.

**Statuses**
- **Pending**: waiting for your manager.
- **Approved**: accepted; it counts in reports.
- **Rejected**: sent back to you with a reason.
- **Draft**: only if your company uses the review workflow; saved but not submitted yet.

**Find entries**
- Use the **Site** and **Period** chips at the top.
- The totals at the top show **Entries** and **tCO₂e entered**. Choose **Pending**, **Approved** or **Rejected** there to show only those.
- Use **Search entries** and the filters; **Clear filters** starts again.
- **Breakdown** shows consumption and emissions by type for one category.

**Open an entry**
Select a row to see its status, who approved or rejected it and when, what was entered, its tCO₂e and its documents.

**Edit a Pending entry**
1. Open the entry and choose **Edit entry**.
2. Change the values. The total updates as you type.
3. Optionally fill in **Reason for the change**, then choose **Save changes**.

**Fix a Rejected entry**
1. Open it. The reason is shown under "Sent back to you".
2. Choose **Fix and resubmit** and correct the values.
3. Say what you changed in **What did you change?** (required). The reviewer sees it.
4. Choose **Resubmit**. It goes back to your manager.

Approved entries can't be edited. If one is wrong, ask your manager.

You can also fix rejected entries from [Current Month](/my-month), or with **Fix** in the list of what's already entered in [Add data](/data/new).
